Subject: Request for Measurement and Demarcation of My Land in Survey No. 18/1A
at Nipani and the 30-Foot-Wide Access Road

Respected Sir,
I am the owner of 11.08 Gunthas of land bearing Survey No. 18/1A situated at Nipani,
along with the 30-foot-wide road provided for access to and from the said property.

For the purpose of measurement and demarcation of the said land and the access road, I had
duly submitted applications to the concerned authorities in the years 2010, 2013 and 2016,
after paying the prescribed measurement fees.

However, despite submitting the applications and paying the requisite measurement fees, the
measurement of the said land and the access road has not been carried out till date. Further,
I have not been provided with any clear reason or written explanation as to why the
measurement has not been conducted.

I, therefore, humbly request you to kindly take up the matter with the concerned authorities and
arrange for the early measurement and proper demarcation of my 11.08-Guntha land in
Survey No. 18/1A and the 30-foot-wide access road. I also request that the official
measurement map/sketch (measurement plan) be prepared and a copy thereof be provided
to me.

I shall be grateful, if necessary, action is taken in this matter at the earliest.

Place: Nipani
Date: 05/09/2026

Yours faithfully,
( Gopal Gajanan Joshi )
