Subject: Compliance with High Court of Karnataka Order in W.P No. 8804 of 2024

Respected Sir/Maam,

I am constrained to address this representation regarding the order dated 21.02.2026 passed by the Hon'ble High Court of Karnataka in Writ Petition No. 8804 of 2024. The Hon'ble Court has taken on record the categorical stand of the Ballari City Corporation that:

(i) the encroached sites have been identified and marked, and

(ii) a General Body Resolution dated 19.10.2024 has already been passed for removal of encroachments and consequential road widening.

Based on the said submissions, the writ petition was disposed of, clearly placing reliance on the assurance and undertaking of the Ballari City Corporation.

Despite the above, it is noted with serious concern that effective action towards removal of encroachments has not been undertaken, thereby defeating the very purpose of the proceedings and the undertaking recorded before the Hon'ble Court.

The encroachments in question pertain to:

- Road stretch from Gangappa Gin towards Mill Road Cross, falling within Ward No 13 and
- Road stretch from Mill Road cross towards Andrai Circle, falling within Ward Nos. 9 and 10,
Ballari.

In the circumstances, you are hereby called upon to forthwith implement the resolution and ensure removal of all encroachments in strict compliance with the statement recorded before the Hon'ble High Court. Kindly note that any continued inaction or delay may amount to non-compliance of the undertaking given to the Hon'ble Court, leaving no option but to initiate appropriate legal proceedings.
