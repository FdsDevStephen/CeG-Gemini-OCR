In a $2$-category, we have objects, morphisms between objects, and $2$-morphisms between morphisms. The composition of morphisms is associative, and there are identity morphisms for each object. Additionally, $2$-morphisms can be composed both vertically and horizontally, satisfying certain coherence conditions.
