The following is a list of the key points discussed in the paper:

1. The concept of a $2$-category is a fundamental structure in higher category theory. It extends the notion of a category by introducing $2$-morphisms between morphisms. In a $2$-category, we have objects, morphisms between objects, and $2$-morphisms between morphisms. The composition of morphisms is associative, and there are identity morphisms for each object. Additionally, $2$-morphisms can be composed both vertically and horizontally, satisfying certain coherence conditions.

2. The study of $2$-categories is crucial for understanding various mathematical structures and their interactions. For instance, the category of categories itself forms a $2$-category, where the objects are categories, the morphisms are functors, and the $2$-morphisms are natural transformations. This example illustrates the power of $2$-categories in unifying and extending the concepts of category theory.

3. The study of $2$-categories also provides insights into the mathematical structures and their interactions. For instance, the category of categories is the category of functors, where the objects are functors, the morphisms are functors, and the $2$-morphisms are natural transformations. This example illustrates the power of $2$-categories in unifying and extending the concepts of category theory.
